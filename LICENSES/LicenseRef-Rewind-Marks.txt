Rewind - brand marks in retro-bauhaus/logos.js

retro-bauhaus/logos.js holds SVG drawings of the logos of the apps that Rewind
opens (streaming services, game consoles). They come from simple-icons
(https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0) and from Wikimedia Commons, as listed in the
file header and in THIRD-PARTY.md. The "navegador" and "hdmi" icons were drawn
for this project and are GPL-3.0-or-later, like the rest of Rewind.
